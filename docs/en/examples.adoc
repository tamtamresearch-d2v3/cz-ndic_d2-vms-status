== Examples of sign configurations

This appendix describes examples of variable message signs (VMS) described by the dynamic and the static
publication. Each example contains:

* the static publication describing the VMS configuration (format `x-format:cz-ndic_d2-vms-table-v1.3`, sample
  `static-…`): an image marking the described parts and their indexes and a description of the basic
  concept,
* one or more dynamic publications (this format, samples `dynamic-…`) describing what is currently
  displayed on the VMS.

The samples of the dynamic publication are part of the specification package `{schema-uri}.zip` (folder
`samples`), the samples of the static publication are part of the `x-format:cz-ndic_d2-vms-table-v1.3` format package.
Displayed texts are given in Czech as in the data, with an English translation.

=== Information cantilever or gantry with ZPI

A cantilever with one traffic information device (PDZ and PIT). The states "information campaign" and "ZPI
out of order" come from other ZPI controllers with the same configuration (one VMS with a pictogram and text).

==== Static publication

image::ex-02.png[Cantilever with ZPI,width=600]

* sample: `static-1-ZPI.xml`
* concept:
** VMS controller (`vms:VmsController`): 1×
** VMS (`vms:Vms`; `roadsideCantileverMounted`): 1× dynamic sign on the cantilever for the whole carriageway [vms:1]
** Display areas (`vms:DisplayArea`): [vms:1] 1× pictogram (with an implicit supplementary panel) and 1× text

(Wilsonova street), "⇒ Muzeum" (⇒ Museum), "práce na silnici" (roadworks); flashers on. The version of

=== Lane control gantry

A lane control gantry with two PDZ on posts along the road and three PDZ for lane control above the road.

==== Static publication

image::ex-04.png[Lane control gantry,width=600]

* sample: `static-2-LM.xml`
* concept:
** VMS controller (`vms:VmsController`): 1×
** VMS (`vms:Vms`; `gantryMounted`):
*** 2× variable sign on the left and right post for the whole carriageway [vms:1,5]
*** 2× dynamic sign on the gantry for lane control [vms:2,4]
*** 1× dynamic sign on the gantry for the whole carriageway [vms:3]
** Display areas (`vms:DisplayArea`):
*** [vms:1,5] 1× pictogram
*** [vms:2,3,4] 1× pictogram (with an implicit supplementary panel)
